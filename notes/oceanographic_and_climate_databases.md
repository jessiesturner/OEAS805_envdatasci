# Oceanographic, Climate, and Environmental Data Resources

A structured directory of field observation databases, time series, remote sensing archives, climate model outputs, and weather/reanalysis data sources from Advanced Environmental Data Science students and instructor Fall 2026, Ocean and Earth Sciences Department, ODU.

---

## 1. Field Data
* **MBON** – [Marine Biodiversity Observation Network](https://marinebon.org/)
* **BCO-DMO** – [Biological and Chemical Oceanography Data Management Office](https://www.bco-dMO.org/)
* **SeaBASS** – [SeaWiFS Bio-optical Archive and Storage System (NASA)](https://seabass.gsfc.nasa.gov/)
* **USGS** – [United States Geological Survey](https://www.usgs.gov/) *(Sediment cores, marsh data)* 
* **Texas A&M Core Repository** – [Gulf Coast Repository (GCR)](https://gcr.tamu.edu/) *Marine geology sediment cores*
* **GBIF** – [Global Biodiversity Information Facility](https://www.gbif.org/)
* **NERRS** – [National Estuarine Research Reserve System](https://coast.noaa.gov/nerrs/)
* **CBP** – [Chesapeake Bay Program Datahub](https://datahub.chesapeakebay.net/)
* **MD-DNR** - [Maryland Department of Natural Resources Eyes on the Bay](https://eyesonthebay.dnr.maryland.gov/)
* **ERP** – [Elizabeth River Project](https://elizabethriver.org/)

---

## 2. Time Series
* **Mauna Loa Observatory** – [Keeling Curve Atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ Record, Hosted by NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory](https://gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/)
* **NOAA Tides & Currents** – [Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/)
* **HOTS** – [Hawaii Ocean Time-series](https://hahana.soest.hawaii.edu/hot/)
* **BATS** – [Bermuda Atlantic Time-series Study](https://bats.bios.edu/)

## 3. Ocean Observing
* **NDBC** – [National Data Buoy Center (NOAA)](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/)
* **GO-SHIP** – [Global Ocean Ship-based Hydrographic Investigations Program](https://www.go-ship.org/)
* **R2R** [Rolling Deck to Repository (UNOLS Vessel Underway Data)](https://www.rvdata.us/)

* **OB.DAAC** – [Ocean Biology Distributed Active Archive Center (NASA)](https://oceancolor.gsfc.nasa.gov/)
* **PO.DAAC** – [Physical Oceanography Distributed Active Archive Center (NASA)](https://podaac.jpl.nasa.gov/)

## 5. Climate Model Output

* **IPCC** – [Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change Data Distribution Centre](https://www.ipcc-data.org/)
* **ECCO** – [Estimating the Circulation and Climate of the Ocean](https://ecco-group.org/)

## 6. Weather / Climate / Reanalysis
* **WDC Climate** – [World Data Center for Climate](https://www.www.dkrz.de/en/services/data-management/wdcc)
* **GLODAP** – [Global Ocean Data Analysis Project](https://www.glodap.info/)
* **NOAA Hurricane Database** – [HURDAT2 / National Hurricane Center](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/)
* **WorldClim** – [Global Climate Data](https://www.worldclim.org/)
* **Airport Data** – [For Example: Norfolk International Airport Data, Hosted by NOAA](https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/cdo-web/datasets/GHCND/stations/GHCND:USW00013737/detail)
* **ERA5** – [ECMWF Climate Reanalysis](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels-monthly-means?tab=download)
* **Windy** – [Windy Interactive Map & Forecasts](https://www.windy.com/)
* **NWS** – [National Weather Service (NOAA)](https://www.weather.gov/)
* **Weather Underground** – [Weather Underground Historical Data](https://www.wunderground.com/history/)
